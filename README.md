Money Manager is a personal finance and daily expense-tracking Android app designed to help you manage your spending, budgets, bills, and financial habits in one place.
What It Does
Tracks Daily Income & Expenses: Log transactions quickly with categories, subcategories, wallets, and notes, and review everything in a clean ledger or calendar view.
Automates Routine Tracking: Detects bank/payment SMS alerts for one-tap transaction recording and automatically logs recurring monthly expenses, EMIs, and salary entries.
Monitors Budgets & Spending Pace: Lets you set category budgets and tracks your daily burn rate, projected monthly spend, and safe daily limit so you can stay on target.
Visualizes Financial Trends: Turns your transactions into charts, monthly reports, spending insights, and habit streaks.
Keeps Your Data Safe: Supports Google Drive backup and automatic sync, plus CSV import/export.
What It Has
Ledger & Calendar Views: Filterable transaction history with daily, monthly, and calendar-based breakdowns.
Budgets & Category Management: Custom income and expense categories with monthly, quarterly, or yearly budget limits and progress tracking.
Analytics, Statistics & Insights: Combined dashboard with category breakdowns, monthly reports, spending velocity/burn rate, and spending habit tracking.
Recurring Bills, EMIs & Salary: Dedicated tracking for recurring payments, EMIs, salary schedules, and borrowed/lent debts.
Financial Habits Tracker: Create daily money habits and track completion streaks.
7 Customizable Home Screen Widgets: Quick Add, Summary, Overview, Monthly Budget, Habits, Velocity, and Spending Habits—all customizable from Settings with Solid or Transparent glass backgrounds, Dynamic Wallpaper Tinting (for the app and/or widgets independently), adjustable inner padding, optional borders (Hairline, Glow, Bold, or Off), and toggleable widget labels.
Themes & Backup: Light, Dark, and System themes with live wallpaper-adaptive Material 3 colors, plus Google Drive sync and local backup/export tools.
